November is **Diabetes Awareness Month**, and this year we're doing something about it.

Kylo's dad, Tom, is going live for **24 hours straight** on his gaming channel, **pobajobs**, to raise money for **Breakthrough T1D**, the charity funding research into type 1 diabetes, better treatments, and one day a cure.

Kylo was diagnosed when he was just 15 months old. Breakthrough T1D funds the research that's already changing life for families like ours, and we'd love to give a little back.

### What we know so far

- **When:** some time in November 2026. We'll confirm the date here soon.
- **Where:** live on [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/pobajobs), with highlights on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@pobajobs).
- **How to donate:** we'll add a donate link closer to the day.

For now, the best way to help is to follow [pobajobs on Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/pobajobs) so you get the alert when the stream goes live, and to share this with anyone who might want to join in.

See you there. Bring snacks. We will.
