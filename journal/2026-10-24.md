We've started something new alongside the journal: **Kyki Kids**, a YouTube channel of short, funny videos about real animals, made for kids and the grown-ups watching with them.

Kyki (say it "Kai-key") is Kylo and Loki mixed together. Each video answers the kind of question a kid would ask (how fast, how big, how many teeth?) with real facts and a silly comparison or two. First up: **10 Amazing Sharks**.

### Meet Squidge

The channel is hosted by Squidge, a lilac octopus with big eyes and plenty of opinions. Look closely and you'll spot a little round sensor on Squidge's arm, just like the one Kylo wears. We wanted kids who wear one to see a character on screen who does too, and for every other kid it's just part of who Squidge is.

There are a few jokes tucked in for the parents as well, so it should survive being played on repeat. Probably.
